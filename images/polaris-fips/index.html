Self-host your music collection, and access it from any computer and mobile device

Polaris is a FOSS music streaming application, designed to let you enjoy your music collection from any computer or mobile device. Polaris works by streaming your music directly from your own computer, without uploading it to a third-party. There are no kind of premium version. The only requirement is that your computer stays on while it streams your music!

Upstream project: github.com/agersant/polaris ↗

polaris-fips

FIPS-compliant version of Polaris. Polaris is an open source policy engine for Kubernetes that validates and remediates resource configuration

# Build the image
nix build .#polaris-fips

nix build .#load-polaris-fips-to-docker && ./result/bin/load-polaris-fips-to-docker
