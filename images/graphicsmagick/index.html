Swiss army knife of image processing

GraphicsMagick is the swiss army knife of image processing, providing a robust and efficient collection of tools and libraries which support reading, writing, and manipulating an image in over 92 major formats including important formats like DPX, GIF, JPEG, JPEG-2000, JXL, PNG, PDF, PNM, TIFF, and WebP.

Upstream project: www.graphicsmagick.org ↗
